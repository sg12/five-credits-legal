Five Credits

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026 (app version 1.0.2)

Five Credits ("the app") is an offline exam-prep app for WAEC, NECO and JAMB candidates. This page explains what happens — and what does not happen — to your data when you use it.

In short: Five Credits does not require an account and does not show ads. Your progress is stored on your device. From version 1.0.2 the app sends usage statistics (which screens and lessons are used, quiz results, session length) linked to a random install ID — never your name, school, phone number, email or anything you type. This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Analytics.

Data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or transmit information that identifies you by name. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in. The app never asks for your name, email, or any identifying information.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Your quiz answers, scores, streaks and study-plan progress are stored in a local database on your device. Usage statistics (see below) are a separate, limited set of events.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access. The app does not request or use any of these.

Local data storage

The app keeps a local, on-device database of your progress (topics completed, question attempts, scores) so it can show you statistics and recommend what to revise next. This data is used only to power features inside the app itself.

Exporting or sharing your own data

Some screens let you manually export your progress (for example, as a CSV or JSON file) using your device's native Share sheet. If you use this feature, you choose where that file goes — for example, your own email or notes app. We never do this automatically, and we never see the exported file ourselves.

Importing content packs

You can import additional content packs from a .zip file you choose yourself. This is a local file-import operation — the app reads the file you selected from your device; it does not upload anything.

Usage statistics (analytics)

To learn which lessons help and where students give up, the app sends usage events to PostHog (posthog.com), an analytics service, using its servers in the European Union. Events are queued on your device and sent when you are online.

  • What is sent: app opened / first opened, onboarding viewed and finished, which tab, subject, topic or lesson was opened, quiz started and finished (topic, score), each answer (question ID, right or wrong, time taken), mock exam started and finished (number of questions, number correct), session length; plus app version, platform (iOS, Android or web), days since install and a random install ID created on first launch.
  • What is never sent: your name, school, class, phone number, email, contacts, location from your device, photos, or any text you type. There is no account, so events cannot be matched to you by name.
  • Network data: like any internet service, PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent and may use it to estimate your country. We do not use it to identify you.
  • Why: to improve lessons and questions and to measure whether students keep using the app (for example, how many return after one or seven days). Not for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone else.
  • Your choice: usage statistics are on by default. Turn them off at any time in Settings → Analytics → "Share usage stats"; after that nothing more is sent. To have the events already sent deleted, email us your request.
  • Retention: events are kept for up to 24 months, then deleted.

The app does not display ads and does not use any advertising SDK or advertising identifier. If this changes, we will update this policy before that update ships and ask for your permission where Apple's or Google's rules require it.

Children's privacy

Five Credits is built for SS3 secondary-school students preparing for WAEC/NECO/JAMB, and is not directed at or marketed to young children. The usage statistics described above contain no name or contact details. If you are under 18, you may want to agree with a parent or guardian whether to keep usage statistics on; you can turn them off in Settings at any time.

Changes to this policy

If we change what data the app collects, we will update this page and the "Privacy Policy" link inside the app's Settings screen before that change ships, and show the date of the change at the top.
