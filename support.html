Support

Five Credits is an offline exam-prep app for WAEC, NECO and JAMB candidates. Need help, found a bug, or have a question about a lesson or question in the app? We'd like to hear from you.

Fastest way to reach us: email nsuemml@gmail.com — include your device model and iOS version if you're reporting a bug, and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

  • The app needs an update / new subjects. Five Credits ships more subjects and topics as content packs — no app update required. Check the Subjects tab for what's currently available.
  • I found a mistake in a question or lesson. Please email us the subject, topic name, and a screenshot if you can — content corrections are one of the most useful reports you can send.
  • Does this app need internet? No — after the initial content pack loads, everything works fully offline.
